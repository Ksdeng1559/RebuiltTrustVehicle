# Product Vision & Trust Model

## Vision

RebuiltTrustVehicle exists to make rebuilt-status vehicles easier to understand, finance, warranty, and transact without hiding their history.

The platform reframes the category around evidence and transparency:

**Rebuilt title + verified evidence + trusted seller + financing/warranty pathways = a more liquid, more understandable market.**

## Trust Layers

### Seller Trust
- authorized dealer verification;
- private seller identity verification;
- seller type shown prominently;
- history of listings and compliance events retained.

### Vehicle Trust
- VIN-centric record;
- rebuilt status disclosed;
- inspection and repair evidence;
- supporting photos and documentation;
- clear provenance for each claim.

### Transaction Trust
- warranty eligibility/status;
- financing pathway;
- standardized disclosures;
- auditable listing changes;
- buyer access to due-diligence documents.

### Platform Trust
The platform does not certify facts it cannot independently verify. It records source, status, and evidence for each material claim.

## Strategic Positioning

RebuiltTrustVehicle should not compete as another general classifieds site. It should become infrastructure for a difficult automotive category where ordinary marketplaces provide too little context.

Long term, the vehicle trust record can become portable and useful across dealer resale, financing, warranty, inspection, and future ownership events.
