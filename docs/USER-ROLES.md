# User Roles & Access Model

## Platform Administrator
Full operational access to approve sellers, review listings, manage trust evidence, view audit logs, and administer platform policies.

## Authorized Dealer
Can manage dealership profile, staff, inventory, vehicle documentation, warranty workflow, financing workflow, leads, and transaction status.

## Dealer Staff
Scoped access delegated by the dealership. Permissions should be configurable by dealership administrators.

## Private Seller
Can create a limited number of listings, upload ownership and vehicle evidence, communicate with buyers, and complete required disclosures. Private sellers receive stricter listing and verification controls than authorized dealers.

## Buyer
Can search listings, review trust records, submit inquiries, request warranty information, begin financing, save vehicles, and access documents shared for due diligence.

## Finance Partner
Receives authorized finance applications and vehicle data required for underwriting. Access is limited to relevant transactions.

## Warranty Partner
Receives VIN and vehicle information necessary to determine eligibility and issue coverage. Warranty partners do not receive unrelated buyer data.

## Inspector / Evidence Provider
May submit or validate inspection documents and related evidence where a verified provider workflow is implemented.

## Principle of Least Privilege
Every account should have access only to the organizations, vehicles, transactions, and personal information necessary for its role.
