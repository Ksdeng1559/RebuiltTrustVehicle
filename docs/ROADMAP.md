# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Repository, PRD, architecture, governance
- Dealer and private seller role model
- VIN-centric listing model
- Trust evidence framework
- Warranty and financing partner discovery
- BC compliance review

## Phase 2 — MVP

- Authentication and multi-tenant organizations
- Dealer onboarding/approval
- Private seller onboarding
- VIN-based listing creation
- Rebuilt disclosure workflow
- Evidence/document uploads
- Buyer search and vehicle detail pages
- Inquiry workflow
- Warranty eligibility handoff
- Financing handoff
- Admin review and audit events

## Phase 3 — Transaction Layer

- Reservations / purchase workflow
- Digital acknowledgements
- Financing status integration
- Warranty contract metadata
- Dealer lead management
- Notifications
- Transaction analytics

## Phase 4 — Trust Network

- Inspection partner network
- Rich repair evidence
- Portable vehicle trust record
- Dealer performance history
- Buyer reviews / dispute operations
- Additional warranty and finance providers

## Phase 5 — Data & Risk Intelligence

- Marketplace pricing intelligence
- Conversion and loss analytics
- Warranty/repair cohort analysis
- Lender-ready vehicle data package
- Dealer inventory recommendations
- Self-funded repair-protection feasibility study, only after legal and actuarial validation

## North-Star Outcome

Make a well-documented rebuilt vehicle materially easier to buy and sell than the same vehicle listed on a generic marketplace with little context.
