# Product Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

- Repository, PRD, architecture, governance
- Authorized dealer, Private Buyer Network, and Private Seller Network role model
- VIN-centric listing model
- Buyer-demand profile model
- Trust evidence framework
- Warranty and financing partner discovery
- Rebuild / Tesla / EV specialist partner model
- BC compliance review

## Phase 2 — MVP

- Authentication and multi-tenant organizations
- Dealer onboarding/approval
- Private buyer onboarding and Vehicle Buying Profiles
- Private seller onboarding
- VIN-based listing creation
- Rebuilt disclosure workflow
- Evidence/document uploads
- Buyer search and vehicle detail pages
- Saved vehicles and buyer preferences
- Initial buyer-to-inventory matching
- Inquiry workflow
- Website AI Buyer Concierge
- CRM lead capture and human handoff
- Warranty eligibility handoff
- Financing handoff
- Admin review and audit events

## Phase 3 — Demand & Transaction Layer

- Buyer match scoring/ranking
- Match alerts and notifications
- AI Sales Agent qualification and follow-up
- AI DM Response Agent
- Dealer matched-opportunity queue
- Reservations / purchase workflow
- Digital acknowledgements
- Financing status integration
- Warranty contract metadata
- Appointment/test-drive workflow
- Dealer lead management
- Transaction analytics

## Phase 4 — Trust Network

- Inspection partner network
- Rebuild / Tesla / EV specialist network
- Rich repair and specialist evidence
- Portable vehicle trust record
- RebuiltTrust EV Verified concept validation
- Dealer performance history
- Buyer reviews / dispute operations
- Additional warranty and finance providers
- Buyer+ / sourcing concierge concept validation

## Phase 5 — Data & Marketplace Intelligence

- Buyer-demand analytics
- Dealer sourcing recommendations based on aggregated demand
- Marketplace pricing intelligence
- Conversion and loss analytics
- Warranty/repair cohort analysis
- Lender-ready vehicle data package
- Match-to-sale optimization
- Dealer inventory recommendations
- Self-funded repair-protection feasibility study, only after legal and actuarial validation

## North-Star Outcome

Make a well-documented rebuilt vehicle materially easier to buy and sell than the same vehicle listed on a generic marketplace with little context, while allowing RebuiltTrustVehicle to connect verified supply with structured buyer demand before, during, and after listing.
