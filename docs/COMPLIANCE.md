# Compliance & Disclosure

## Core Principle

RebuiltTrustVehicle must improve transparency rather than obscure rebuilt history.

## Required Disclosure Principles

- Rebuilt status is prominent on every applicable listing.
- Seller type is clearly identified.
- Warranty eligibility is not represented as active coverage.
- Financing availability is not represented as guaranteed approval.
- Platform trust indicators are evidence/completeness signals, not government or mechanical certification.
- Material seller claims should identify their source.
- Buyers should be able to access required disclosures before completing a transaction.

## Private Seller Controls

Private participation requires identity and ownership verification, listing limits, and monitoring intended to prevent the private channel from becoming an unlicensed dealer channel.

## Data & Privacy

Sensitive identity and finance data should be minimized, access-controlled, encrypted where appropriate, and retained only according to documented operational/legal requirements.

## Regulatory Workstream

Before launch, validate applicable BC requirements concerning:
- motor vehicle dealer licensing and advertising;
- rebuilt vehicle representations;
- consumer protection;
- financing referrals and compensation;
- warranty/service contract sales and compensation;
- private seller activity and transaction limits;
- privacy and consent;
- electronic agreements and records.

This wiki is a product design record and not legal advice.
