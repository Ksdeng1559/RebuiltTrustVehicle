# Rebuilt Vehicle Trust Framework

## Objective

Create a standardized, evidence-based representation of a rebuilt-status vehicle without implying a guarantee of mechanical condition.

## Evidence Categories

### Identity
- VIN
- year / make / model / trim
- odometer
- seller identity
- ownership evidence where applicable

### Status
- rebuilt status prominently disclosed
- source and date of status information recorded

### Repair History
- damage description where available
- repair invoices
- body/frame repair records
- parts records
- before/after photos
- seller disclosure

### Inspection Evidence
- required inspections
- mechanical inspection
- alignment/frame evidence where relevant
- inspector identity and date

### Transaction Evidence
- warranty eligibility/status
- financing eligibility/status
- asking price
- buyer acknowledgements

## Provenance Types

Every material field should carry a provenance label:
- authoritative/official source;
- third-party professional;
- seller supplied;
- platform derived.

## Trust Status

A platform trust score/status should measure evidence completeness and verification workflow completion, not predict future reliability.

Suggested states:
- Basic Disclosure
- Evidence Added
- Verified Seller + Evidence
- High Documentation Completeness

Avoid terms such as "certified safe" or "guaranteed reliable" unless backed by an appropriately authorized third party.

## Buyer Presentation

The vehicle page should show:
1. rebuilt status first;
2. what happened, if known;
3. what was repaired;
4. who inspected it;
5. what evidence is available;
6. warranty status;
7. financing availability;
8. unresolved or missing information.
