# Dealer Onboarding

## Objective

Allow only verified, authorized used-car dealers to access the primary seller channel.

## Required Onboarding Steps

1. Create organization account.
2. Verify business identity.
3. Verify dealer licensing/authorization information.
4. Verify primary contact.
5. Accept marketplace terms and rebuilt-vehicle disclosure rules.
6. Configure dealership profile and locations.
7. Add staff with role-based permissions.
8. Configure financing and warranty participation preferences.
9. Complete first-listing quality review.
10. Activate seller account.

## Review States

- Started
- Documents required
- Under review
- Additional information required
- Approved
- Suspended
- Rejected

## Ongoing Controls

Dealer verification is not permanent. The platform should support periodic re-verification, expired-license alerts, complaint review, listing audits, and suspension workflows.

## Dealer Value Proposition

The onboarding burden should be justified by higher-trust positioning, buyer leads, warranty access, financing pathways, standardized evidence handling, and faster transaction conversion.
