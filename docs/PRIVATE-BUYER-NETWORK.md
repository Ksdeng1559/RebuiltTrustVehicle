# Private Buyer Network

## Purpose

The Private Buyer Network is the demand-side engine of RebuiltTrustVehicle. It is designed for consumers who intentionally want to buy rebuilt-status vehicles for personal use, particularly newer and late-model vehicles, premium vehicles, EVs, and rebuilt Teslas.

The objective is to capture buyer intent before inventory appears and match that demand to verified dealer inventory and eligible private-seller vehicles.

## Strategic Role

Traditional marketplaces depend on a passive sequence:

**Vehicle listed → buyer discovers it → buyer inquires.**

RebuiltTrustVehicle adds a proactive sequence:

**Buyer joins → defines desired vehicle → demand is structured → inventory appears → AI matches → dealer receives qualified opportunity.**

This allows the platform to organize both vehicle supply and buyer demand.

## Buyer Profile

A buyer may create one or more Vehicle Buying Profiles containing explicit preferences such as:

- make;
- model;
- model-year range;
- maximum purchase price;
- target monthly payment;
- down payment;
- financing requirement;
- warranty requirement/preference;
- trade-in indicator;
- EV preference;
- acceptable mileage;
- geographic preference;
- purchase timeframe;
- required features;
- optional preferences.

Explicit buyer requirements must remain distinguishable from AI-inferred preferences.

## Matching Engine

The matching engine evaluates active buyer profiles against:

- current authorized-dealer inventory;
- incoming authorized-dealer inventory;
- eligible Private Seller Network inventory;
- specialist-reviewed inventory where available.

A match record should identify:

- hard criteria satisfied;
- hard criteria not satisfied;
- optional preferences satisfied;
- source of each buyer requirement;
- match status;
- buyer notification status;
- dealer routing status;
- AI/human disposition.

The system should be able to explain why a vehicle was recommended.

## Buyer Experience

Private buyers can:

- browse public inventory;
- create and manage buyer profiles;
- save vehicles and searches;
- receive matching alerts;
- compare Vehicle Trust Records;
- ask the AI Buyer Concierge questions;
- request warranty information;
- request financing/pre-qualification workflows;
- identify trade-in interest;
- schedule appointments or test drives;
- access due-diligence documents made available by the seller;
- pause or delete demand profiles subject to retention requirements.

## AI Sales Workflow

Buyer profile or inbound inquiry → AI Sales Agent identifies missing requirements → matching engine evaluates inventory → buyer receives relevant recommendations → high-intent opportunity routed to authorized dealer → CRM updated → financing/warranty/appointment workflows initiated → human takeover where needed.

The AI must not claim:
- financing approval without partner confirmation;
- warranty coverage without eligibility/contract confirmation;
- mechanical quality without authoritative evidence;
- specialist verification that has not occurred.

## Dealer Value

The Private Buyer Network provides authorized dealers with:

- pre-qualified demand signals;
- buyer preference trends;
- matched opportunities;
- faster response to newly listed inventory;
- sourcing intelligence;
- better lead context;
- higher potential conversion versus anonymous listing traffic.

Aggregated demand intelligence may eventually help dealers understand what rebuilt vehicles buyers actually want, subject to privacy and consent requirements.

- match rate;
- buyer alert open/response rate;
- matched inquiry rate;
- qualified-opportunity rate;
- appointment rate from matches;
- financing application rate from matches;
- warranty attachment rate from matches;
- match-to-sale conversion;
- median time from match to dealer response;
- buyer profile reactivation rate;
- buyer satisfaction/trust score.

## Product Principle

The Private Buyer Network should make RebuiltTrustVehicle a demand-driven marketplace rather than a passive classifieds site. Buyer intent is valuable platform infrastructure, but it must remain transparent, consent-aware, explainable, and clearly separated from inferred data.
